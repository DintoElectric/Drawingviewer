Dinto Electric · internal tool concept

Drawings hub — tablet mockups

Five screens for viewing electrical drawings in the field and keeping revisions straight. Built for a tablet in landscape (1194×834), one polished direction. Assumptions: sheets live under project → discipline → sheet number; a foreman only ever needs the current rev unless they ask to compare; a new upload is matched to the sheet it supersedes automatically, and a human confirms. Drawing areas are grey placeholders — drop in real sheet exports when you have them.
